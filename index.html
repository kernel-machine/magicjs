🎨 Akai APC Mini MK2 LED Synchronization

This software automates the synchronization of LED colors on the Akai APC Mini MK2 controller with button colors in the QLC+ Virtual Console.

Load your QLC+ workspace file (.qxw) and the system will automatically analyze the colors of buttons in the graphical interface, finding the closest MIDI color match to configure the controller LEDs with the same colors.
